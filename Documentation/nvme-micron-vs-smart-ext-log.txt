nvme-micron-vs-smart-ext-log(1)
===============================

NAME
----
nvme-micron-vs-smart-ext-log - Retrieve extended SMART logs

SYNOPSIS
--------
[verse]
'nvme' [<global-options>] 'micron vs-smart-ext-log' <device>

DESCRIPTION
-----------
Retrieve extended SMART logs.

The <device> parameter is mandatory and may be either the NVMe character
device (ex: /dev/nvme0), or a namespace block device (ex: /dev/nvme0n1).
