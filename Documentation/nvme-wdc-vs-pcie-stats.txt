NAME
----
nvme-wdc-vs-pcie-stats - WDC VS PCIE Statistics

SYNOPSIS
--------
[verse]
'nvme' [<global-options>] 'wdc vs-pcie-stats' <device>

DESCRIPTION
-----------
WDC VS PCIE Statistics.

The <device> parameter is mandatory and may be either the NVMe character
device (ex: /dev/nvme0), or a namespace block device (ex: /dev/nvme0n1).
