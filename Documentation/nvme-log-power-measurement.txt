nvme-log-power-measurement(1)
=============================

NAME
----
nvme-log-power-measurement - Retrieve Power Measurement Log, show it

SYNOPSIS
--------
[verse]
'nvme' [<global-options>] 'log power-measurement' <device>
			[--raw-binary | -b]

DESCRIPTION
-----------
Retrieve Power Measurement Log, show it.

The <device> parameter is mandatory and may be either the NVMe character
device (ex: /dev/nvme0), or a namespace block device (ex: /dev/nvme0n1).
