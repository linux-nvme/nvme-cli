nvme-micron-clear-fw-activate-history(1)
========================================

NAME
----
nvme-micron-clear-fw-activate-history - Clear FW activation history

SYNOPSIS
--------
[verse]
'nvme' [<global-options>] 'micron clear-fw-activate-history' <device>

DESCRIPTION
-----------
Clear FW activation history.

The <device> parameter is mandatory and may be either the NVMe character
device (ex: /dev/nvme0), or a namespace block device (ex: /dev/nvme0n1).
