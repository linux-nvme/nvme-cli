nvme-micron-vs-cloud-log(1)
===========================

NAME
----
nvme-micron-vs-cloud-log - Retrieve Extended Health Information of Hyperscale NVMe Boot SSD

SYNOPSIS
--------
[verse]
'nvme' [<global-options>] 'micron vs-cloud-log' <device>

DESCRIPTION
-----------
Retrieve Extended Health Information of Hyperscale NVMe Boot SSD.

The <device> parameter is mandatory and may be either the NVMe character
device (ex: /dev/nvme0), or a namespace block device (ex: /dev/nvme0n1).
